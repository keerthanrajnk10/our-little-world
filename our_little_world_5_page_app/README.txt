OUR LITTLE WORLD — FIVE-PAGE PERSONAL LOVE APP

PUBLISH:
1. Open your GitHub repository: https://github.com/kerthanrajnk-ops/tulip-surprise
2. Upload and replace index.html, and also upload manifest.json and sw.js to the repository root.
3. Commit changes and wait 1–2 minutes.
4. Open https://kerthanrajnk-ops.github.io/tulip-surprise/

PERSONALIZE:
1. Open the website and tap "Edit our pages".
2. Add your own text, photos and video in the editor areas on each page.
3. Tap Save, then Finish editing.
4. Use Next buttons to move through pages 1–5. Page 5 has no next button.

IMPORTANT:
Text is saved in this browser's local storage. Uploaded photos and video are saved in this browser's IndexedDB on this device. They do not automatically sync to her phone or other devices. To share your edited story, this version needs the same uploaded media files and text to be published with the site. Keep backups of your originals and do not clear browser site data.
For a short video, keep the file size manageable for phone loading.

INSTALL:
After publishing, open the site in Android Chrome, tap the three-dot menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen if offered.
